Reading
  The 5-min trace is the no-voltage trace scaled down plus ~20 % of the empty-cuvette pulse at the
  empty-cuvette delay: part of the beam no longer crosses tissue. The dip near 0.56 THz is where these
  two paths cancel. One-path changes of the tissue fit worse. The mechanism that opens the air path
  (gas, the slice drying or pulling away from the window, beam or sample moving) is not identified.
