Reading
  The 5-min trace contains ~21 % of the empty-cuvette pulse at the empty-cuvette delay (+-0.02 ps): a
  component that crosses at most ~20 % of the tissue's optical thickness. Without it nothing fits (94 %).
  With it, the tissue path must also change: 0.59 x in amplitude, or ~10 % more optical thickness, which
  gives the dip at 0.57 THz with 10.7 of 11.4 dB. Neither the cause of the undelayed component (gas,
  slice pulling away from a window, beam or sample movement) nor of the tissue change is identified;
  the sum is rejected as an exact description (residual 8x tracing jitter).
